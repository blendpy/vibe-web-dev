Clinical & Educational Screening Disclaimer

This platform and cognitive screening battery are designed strictly for educational, research, and preventive awareness. The cognitive tasks are digital adaptations inspired by validated neuropsychological screening batteries (Mini-Cog, MoCA, AD8, Trail Making Test), not a formal medical diagnosis of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) or Alzheimer’s disease. Performance can be influenced by acute stress, lack of sleep, sensory impairments, or test fatigue. If you or family members notice persistent memory difficulties or daily functional impairment, please schedule an evaluation with a board-certified neurologist or geriatrician.
