How to Play Memory Match Solitaire

1. Core Objective: Flip two cards at a time to find matching emoji pairs. When a pair matches, both cards smoothly fade out to reveal their portion of the hidden picture underneath.

2. Wild Joker Tile (3×3 & 5×5): Odd-sized grids include a single 🃏 Wild Card that automatically reveals its tile as soon as you flip it!

3. Random 50% Hint Flash: Clicking Hint Flash (or pressing H) briefly flashes half of all remaining unmatched tiles. Adjust the flash duration from 0.1s to 2.0s using the slider.

4. Pause & Hide (Spacebar): Press Space at any time to pause the clock and conceal the board.

5. Custom Photos & Plants: Click Upload Image or Random Plant (fruit, fern, cactus, fungus) to add custom images to the top of your artwork list.
