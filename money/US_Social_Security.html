How Benefits Are Calculated

Social Security calculates your Primary Insurance Amount (PIA) based on your top 35 highest-earning years (adjusted for wage growth). If you work fewer than 35 years, $0 earnings are factored into your Average Indexed Monthly Earnings (AIME).

Taxation of SS Benefits

Depending on your combined provisional income (AGI + nontaxable interest + 50% of SS benefit), up to 50% or 85% of your benefits may be subject to federal income tax:
• Single: $25k–$34k (50%), >$34k (85%)
• Joint: $32k–$44k (50%), >$44k (85%)

Working in Retirement

If you claim early before Full Retirement Age (67) and continue working, $1 is withheld for every $2 earned above the annual earnings limit ($22,320). Once you reach FRA, no earnings test applies and withheld benefits are recalculated into higher payouts.
